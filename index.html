    ABOUT AQUACHECK

    Simple. Explainable. Useful.

    AquaCheck is a prototype AI reasoning system designed to connect water-related observations with reported symptoms and generate an understandable contamination risk alert.

    Instead of treating water evidence and health observations separately, AquaCheck combines both evidence streams through predefined rules.

    The purpose is to provide a quick first alert and encourage appropriate water-quality testing when concerning patterns are identified.
